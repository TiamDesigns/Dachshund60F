# Shared 3D models

Models the stock KiCad 10 library doesn't have, used by more than one board.
Footprints reference them as `${KIPRJMOD}/../3dmodels/<file>`. Colours are in the STEP files.

- `USB_C_Receptacle_HRO_TYPE-C-31-M-12.step`: generated by `gen_models.py`, not the
  manufacturer's model. An 8.94 × 3.26 mm stadium shell, 7.3 mm deep (the footprint's fab outline), open
  at the front with the standard 8.34 × 2.56 mm opening. Inside: the 6.69 × 0.6 mm tongue with 12 contacts
  a side, and the insulator with its two locating posts. Outside: the four shell legs in the footprint's
  slots, reaching 1.0 mm below the board's top face, and the 12 SMD tails over their pads. The legs'
  length and the tongue's recess (0.85 mm) are estimates. The footprints place it with no offset or
  rotation. On both boards the shell's front overhangs the board edge by 1.3 mm.
- `Bourns_PEC12R-2x30F-S_standin.step`: generated by `gen_models.py`, for the roller encoder SW3: Bourns'
  PEC12R-2xxxF-S family (shaft parallel to the board, push switch) with the 30 mm shaft (PEC12R-2230F-S0024;
  the shaft length is a parameter of `bourns_pec12r_2xf_s()`). Numbers from the
  PEC12R-2xxxF-Sxxxx drawing in Bourns' datasheet (REV. 07/21):
  - shaft axis 10.0 mm above the board's top face, over pad C
  - body 12.5 wide × 13.4 tall, centred on the axis (so 3.3 mm above the board, top at 16.7), 6.0 deep from
    the rear face (switch block included) to the front face, in a metal frame with legs down into the
    mounting-tab holes and feet under the rear corners
  - hub Ø7.0 × 5.0; shaft Ø6.0 with a 4.5 mm D-flat over the last 12.0 mm (F) and a 0.5 mm tip chamfer,
    30.0 mm (L) from the rear face to the tip
  - the five pins, 0.8 mm wide, 3.5 mm below the board

  The footprint (`RotaryEncoder_Bourns_Horizontal_PEC12R-2x17F-Sxxxx`) matches the drawing's hole pattern.
  It places the model with no offset or rotation: the body's front face at footprint x = -2.5 (its fab
  outline), the rear face at +3.5, the hub to -7.5 and the tip at -26.5. The axis-to-body-bottom split
  (6.7 / 6.7) and the body depth's start point are read off the drawing, not dimensioned; the frame, feet
  and legs are simplified. Bourns' own STEP (componentsearchengine.com, needs a free account) would settle
  them.
- `JST_PH_S2B-PH-SM4-TB_standin.step`: generated by `gen_models.py`, for the battery connector J2.
  The housing is the footprint's fab outline (7.9 × 6.0 mm, with the two rear guides beside the tails),
  4.8 mm tall, with the plug cavity, the latch window on top, two 0.5 mm square contacts bent down to
  their SMD tails, and the two metal fittings on the MP pads. The cavity size and the lead path are
  approximate. Replace it with JST's STEP (jst-mfg.com, emailed after you accept their licence) if you
  need it exact. JST's licence forbids passing that file on, so don't commit it to a public repo.

## Regenerating

`gen_models.py` builds the USB-C, JST and encoder models with FreeCAD (tested with 1.1):

```
"C:/Program Files/FreeCAD 1.1/bin/python.exe" hardware/3dmodels/gen_models.py
```

Each model is drawn in its footprint's frame, so footprints use it with zero offset and rotation. Then
rerun `python hardware/export_step.py`.
